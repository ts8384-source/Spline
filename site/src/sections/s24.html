5 · Interpolating cubic splines

This slide is one concrete recipe built from everything before it. The problem: you are given data points and want the smooth curve that passes through all of them. The answer: a cubic spline (degree 3, so C² with ordinary knots) whose knots are the data points' x-values. On each interval is a cubic with four coefficients, so intervals have unknowns. Interpolation and smoothness supply conditions, and two boundary conditions fill the gap. (This matches slide 3: such a spline has free numbers, the data points use up of them, and exactly 2 are left over.) Drag the points, pick a boundary type, and hover each part of the equations.
