5 · What is t? A clock

Think of the red dot as a car on a road and as the clock on the dashboard, 0 to 1. The curve is just the recording of where the car was at each tick. The coloured dots are ticks every 0.1 of the clock. On the right, the same trip is drawn as two ordinary graphs against : one for the x position and one for the height.

The ticks are equally spaced in time but not on the road: the clock is steady, the car is not. The yellow line is the car's velocity arrow: speed = 3 × its length. Drag P1 far from P0 and watch the first ticks spread out.
