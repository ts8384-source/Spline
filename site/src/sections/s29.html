4 · Why C^(n−1)? One free number per knot

Take two neighbouring pieces of a spline of degree that agree in value and in the first derivatives at their shared knot. Their difference is a polynomial of degree at most that is flat to order at the knot, so it must be a single power, . That leaves exactly one free number per knot: the jump in the n-th derivative.
