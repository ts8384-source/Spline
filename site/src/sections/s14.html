11 · Knots are the seams of a curve

A spline curve is several pieces joined end to end. Each colour below is one piece. The strip underneath is the clock , cut into the same colours. A knot is the moment on the clock when the curve switches from one piece to the next, so on the curve it shows up as a seam. Drag a knot on the clock and watch its seam slide along the curve. Drag the grey control points to reshape it.

At the selected (yellow) seam, the wide arrows show how the piece on the left arrives and the narrow arrows show how the piece on the right leaves. Yellow is velocity and red is acceleration. Step the copies from 1 to 4:

1 copy (C²): both pairs of arrows coincide, so the seam is invisible.

2 copies (C¹): the yellow arrows still coincide but the red ones split: the path is smooth, the bend changes abruptly.

3 copies (C⁰): the yellow arrows split too: a corner. Notice the curve now passes exactly through a control point.

4 copies: the curve can jump: two seam dots appear, one for each side.
