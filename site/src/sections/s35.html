3 · Hermite as a fixed matrix on a window

Ask for the curve at 8 evenly spaced sample times (frames 0 … 7), with three knots at the first sample, the middle of the window and the last sample. The six numbers are an offset and a slope at each knot (slopes are "change per sample"). Because the curve is linear in those six numbers, it is again one fixed matrix times , like the B-spline matrix on the Math page. Move the sliders, or pick a preset, and watch both the curve and the product.

The matrix is computed once from the Hermite bumps and never depends on . Sample 0 and sample 7 sit exactly on knots, so their rows pick out one offset each. The middle knot falls between samples 3 and 4, so no row equals that offset alone. Compared with 6 B-spline control points, the six numbers here mean something you can read off directly (a height and a slope at three places), but the curve is only C¹ at the middle knot.
