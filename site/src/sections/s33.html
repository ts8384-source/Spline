1 · One piece: two points and two slopes

A cubic Hermite piece is a cubic that you describe by what you want at its two ends: the value and the slope (the tangent) at each end. On the clock that is four numbers: at the start and at the end. The curve is those four numbers times four fixed cubic bumps . Drag the dots up and down to set the values, and drag the small handles to tilt the slopes. Hover a symbol in the equation to see its term.

Top: the curve; dots are the values at the two ends, the coloured handles tilt the slopes. Lower: the four terms that add up to the curve (white). Each bump has a job: is 1 at the start with flat slope, is 1 at the end with flat slope, has slope 1 at the start and value 0 at both ends, has slope 1 at the end and value 0 at both ends. That is why the four numbers mean exactly what they say.
