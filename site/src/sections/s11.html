7 · Position, velocity, acceleration: a refresher

Three words for how a moving dot behaves, all measured against the clock . Position is where it is. Velocity is how its position is changing (which way and how fast). Acceleration is how the velocity itself is changing. Pick a motion and slide the clock. The acceleration arrow is split into two thinner parts: along the path (changes the speed) and sideways (bends the path).
