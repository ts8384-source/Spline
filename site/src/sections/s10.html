8 · What C⁰, C¹ and C² feel like

Three joins, same left piece. A ball rides the whole curve on the clock (0 to 1 on the blue piece, 1 to 2 on the green one). The yellow arrow is its velocity. The red arrow is its acceleration: the push you would feel. The dashed circles at the join are the circles that each side is bending along. A smaller circle means a tighter bend.

C⁰: the yellow arrow flips direction as the ball passes the join: the ball turns on the spot, so it is a corner.

C¹: the yellow arrow carries on smoothly, but the red arrow suddenly changes, and the two circles have different sizes. It is like holding the steering wheel at one angle and then snapping it to another: no corner, but a jolt.

C²: the red arrow also carries on smoothly, and both sides share the same circle. The bend changes gradually, so the ride is smooth.
