4 · Where do the weights come from? A pouring board

Drop 1 unit of weight at the top. At every node it splits: the fraction goes down-left (blue) and the fraction goes down-right (yellow). Where it ends up under each control point is that point's weight in .

Click any circle to see exactly how its number is made (the two white lines flow into it), or click a bottom point to light up every route that reaches it. Set t = 0.5 and 5 points to check the quartic question yourself: at 0.5 every route weighs the same, so a point's weight is just how many routes lead to it. Slide t to 0 or 1 and all the weight piles onto one end.
